A few examples should show the power of *SPS*:

```apl
      M SPS 2,[1.5]⍳5        ⍝ ROW 2; COLS 1-5
8 9 10 11 12

      M SPS(⍳5),[1.5] 2      ⍝ ROWS 1-5; COL 2
2 9 16 23 30

      M SPS 5 1⍴⍳5            ⍝ LAST COORD. EXTENDED
1 9 17 25 33
```
